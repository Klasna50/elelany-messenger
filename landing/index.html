Good to know

Frequently asked

Is Elelany free?+
Yes. Elelany is free to use on the web and as a desktop app for Mac and Windows. Just create an account and start chatting.
Which devices does it run on?+
Elelany works in any modern browser and installs as a native-feeling desktop app on macOS and Windows. You can also add the web app to your home screen. One account keeps everything in sync.
Can I make voice and video calls?+
Absolutely. One-to-one voice and video calls run over WebRTC — peer-to-peer for low latency. Start a call from any chat with a single tap. Calls work across most networks worldwide.
How does privacy work?+
Elelany is private by default. You only become visible in someone's contacts once you already share a conversation, so your details aren't exposed to strangers. Your contacts are never sold or shared.
Do I have to update the desktop app manually?+
No. The desktop app updates itself quietly in the background and lets you know when a new version is ready — just restart and you're on the latest release.
